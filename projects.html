Engineering Standards

The Titan 5-Stage Build Protocol

Every engine overhaul and custom build follows a zero-compromise assembly process.

STAGE 01
Teardown & Clean

Ultrasonic parts wash, magnaflux crack testing, and bore measurement.

STAGE 02
Precision Machining

Torque-plate diamond honing, deck resurfacing, and crankshaft line-boring.

STAGE 03
Forged Assembly

Dynamic rotating assembly balancing and ARP Custom Age stud fastening.

STAGE 04
Pre-Lube & Cold Run

External oil pump pressurization to prime all oil galleries before initial crank.

STAGE 05
Dyno Break-In

Loaded dyno heat-cycling and spectrographic oil analysis verification.
