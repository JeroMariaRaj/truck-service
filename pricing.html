Cost Determinants

Factors Affecting Final Repair Costs

Unlike passenger car repairs, heavy diesel service pricing depends on several critical mechanical factors:

  • Engine Platform & Accessibility: A V8 diesel (like the Ford 6.7L Powerstroke) requires cab-off access for major long-block work, whereas an inline-6 (like the Cummins 6.7L or Detroit DD13) allows in-frame access without cab removal.
  • Component Contamination: If a high-pressure CP4 fuel pump fails and sheds metal filings into the fuel system, the entire fuel system (all 8 injectors, high-pressure rails, lines, and tank) must be replaced or chemically flushed.
  • OEM vs Billet Upgrades: Utilizing competition-grade ARP Custom Age 625+ head studs or billet torque converters increases parts cost but guarantees longevity under high boost.
