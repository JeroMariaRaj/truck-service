Operational Philosophy

Why We Refuse to 'Guess and Replace'

Too many repair shops treat heavy-duty diesels like standard gasoline cars, plugging in a handheld scanner and replacing sensors blindly. When a modern common rail diesel suffers from a rail pressure dip, an improperly diagnosed $3,000 high-pressure pump replacement won't fix an injector command valve that is bleeding fuel internally.

At Titan, every diagnosis follows strict OEM root-cause isolation: physical relative cylinder compression testing, return-leakage quantity measurement, oscilloscope wave-form analysis, and borescope cylinder inspections. We provide photographic evidence to you before turning a single bolt.
